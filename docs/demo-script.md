# Demo video script

Target length 2:40, hard limit under 3:00. Public YouTube, with audio; narration may be your own voice or text-to-speech. No title card: the rules ask for the project working in the first 10 to 15 seconds. Record already logged in, in a fresh window, at a desktop viewport of at least 1400 px so the agent panel sits beside the page. Do not use third-party trademarks or copyrighted music.

Setup before recording: Chrome 149+ with `chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing` enabled (verified path), or the ChatGPT desktop app's built-in browser in a Work or Codex chat with GPT-5.6 Sol or Terra. Open https://webmcp-simulator.vercel.app/, click "Try the HeatFlow demo", then "Launch simulation", and confirm the panel says "Connected" with five tools before you press record.

Prompts that produce the expected results:

- "Find heat pump services" → two cards remain.
- "Compare the air-source and the ground-source heat pump" → comparison panel.
- "Is an air-source heat pump available at postcode 2230?" → "Service available".
- "Prepare a quote request for an air-source heat pump, postcode 2230, 150 square metres, message: replacing an old gas boiler. Do not send it." → form filled, nothing sent.
